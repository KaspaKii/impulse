Help: before you send anything
  • Read the four destinations before you fund: the role check does not say who controls a pinned script.
  • Fund only your own leg: the seller the asset vault, the buyer the cash vault.
  • Check the amount in KAS and in sompi (1 KAS is 100000000 sompi) in your wallet before you send.
  • A KCC-20 leg needs two outputs, the token and a little KAS: this page gives the vault address only.
  • Wallets here live in this page's memory only: reload, close or Wipe destroys the key and any KAS in it.
  • This page funds the buyer's KAS cash leg, and builds, signs and submits the settle, the Direct trade and the refund.
Details

The role check ran for the role and key you gave: your two destinations pay your key or equal the pins you gave, and neither pays the counterparty or the authority. It does not say who controls a script you pinned, and it does not read the token. Read the four destinations above.

Only the depositor of a leg funds its vault: the seller the asset vault, the buyer the cash vault. The page gives a funding URI and a QR code only for your own leg. Send nothing to the other vault.

The funding URI states the amount in sompi (1 KAS is 100000000 sompi). Whether your wallet reads the amount as sompi is not yet checked against real wallets: confirm the wallet shows exactly the amount in KAS and sompi given here before you send.

A KCC-20 leg is funded with two outputs, the escrowed token and a little KAS; no single URI can say that. This build gives the vault address and the token owner only.

The wallets in this page exist in this page's memory only. Reloading or closing the page, or pressing Wipe, destroys the key, and any KAS in that wallet is then unrecoverable. This page has no control that shows or exports a key, so it cannot back one up. Whatever a trade pays to a wallet's key cannot be spent from this page except as a fee input or token input of another transaction it builds. A deposit the buyer funds into a cash vault from a wallet can be taken back by the buyer only with a refund signed by that wallet's key (from the expiry, in earmarked mode), so closing the page or wiping that wallet before the trade settles leaves the refund to the seller's or the authority's goodwill.

This page funds one thing: the buyer's KAS cash leg, from a wallet of this page, with the wallet's own UTXOs. It does not fund a KCC-20 leg; the seller does that with the command line. It also builds, signs and submits the settle, the Direct trade and the refund, from the UTXOs the connected node reports. Whether a real node accepts what it builds has not been checked: the wire forms are written from the source of the node software and tested against an offline stand-in.

This page refuses a trade whose declared KAS value, or whose native KAS leg, is above 10.00000000 KAS. Non-custodial software, not advice or an offer.

Details

For a token leg the value is the figure you declare, and nothing checks it. Nothing here is advice or an offer. Impulse is non-custodial, issues no asset or token, and does not check issuer terms or sanctions obligations; those stay your own responsibility. Use only funds you can afford to lose.

Details

The page talks to one node: the wRPC JSON address you type (a kaspad started with --rpclisten-json). It asks it for the DAA score and for the UTXOs of the addresses it names, and sends it one more thing, the signed transaction you submit. Nothing else leaves this page, and there is no default node.

This page's policy allows no other plaintext link than ws:// to a kaspad on this machine, so a node reachable over the internet needs wss://.

Details

Two wallets made in this page, with keys the page draws from your browser's randomness. A key is held in memory only: this page's code never stores, shows, sends or logs a key, and has no button that exports one (the browser and the engine may keep copies of the bytes they handled, which the page cannot clear). It is wiped when you press Wipe, and when this page is hidden or closed. Pressing Wipe asks first when the last balance you read was above zero, and when the wallet funded a cash vault and this page has not seen that trade settled or refunded: the wallet's key is then the buyer's own way to refund the deposit, and a wipe destroys it.

A wallet is a signing key and a source of fee inputs, not a place to keep KAS. This page has no button that sends KAS from a wallet to an address you type: the only transactions it builds are the trade's own (the funding of the cash leg, the settle, the Direct trade, the refund). Whatever a trade pays to a wallet's key (the delivered token, the proceeds, a refund, the change) can be spent from this page only as an input of another transaction it builds, and is lost for good when the page is closed. So on mainnet the SDK refuses any destination of your role, and any change, that pays a key of these wallets: pin an address you control elsewhere for each of your destinations, and give a change address. On testnet it is allowed, and the result says so. Send a wallet only what it needs for fees.

Creation and value

Details

There is no default: read the current virtual DAA score from the node you connected to, or type it. A typed score is marked as typed, and a stale one makes the time left to expiry look longer than it is. Mainnet refuses a typed score; it takes only one the connected node gave, unedited.

The KAS value is your own figure: nothing on chain or in this page checks it. It is cleared each time terms are loaded, so every trade needs a fresh declaration.

04Role check: you

Say which party you are. Before it shows you anything to fund, the SDK checks that the two destinations of your role pay your key (or the destination you pin), and never the counterparty or the authority: the contract lets those parties trigger a refund, which pays your leg to your refund destination as the terms write it.

Pins: a destination you control that is not your key (a custodian or multisig)

A Kaspa address of the terms' network in canonical form, or kcc20:pubkey:<hex>, kcc20:script_hash:<hex>, kcc20:covenant_id:<hex>. A pinned destination must equal the pin exactly. A destination that pays the counterparty or the authority is refused whatever you pin.

The other party's pins, when a wallet of this page is the other party

Only for a trade where this page holds both parties' keys (for example a Direct trade between wallet A and wallet B): the wallet that is the other party signs under these pins, as yours signs under the ones above. They are given to no other wallet.

      05Fund the cash leg
      
    Buyer only, after the seller's asset leg is listed by the node. Closing or reloading this page, or a wipe, destroys the wallet key that refunds the deposit.
    
      
    
      
    
        Details
        
    Only the buyer funds a KAS cash vault, with one output of exactly the cash amount. The wallet chosen as fee payer above pays it, and the fee, from its own UTXOs; it must hold the buyer's key. The change goes to the change address, or back to the wallet's key. A KCC-20 leg is not funded here: the seller funds the asset leg with the command line (impulse fund build), and the page funds no token whose template is not a pinned kcc20.sil build (SDK_TEMPLATE_UNPINNED, no override).
    
        
    Fund it after the seller's asset leg has been accepted, not before. The page asks the node for the asset vault and the token escrowed in it and refuses to build the funding while the seller's asset leg is not listed (SDK_FUND_ASSET_NOT_FUNDED), and when the cash vault already holds a deposit of exactly the cash amount (SDK_FUND_ALREADY_FUNDED). Until the trade settles, your only way to take the deposit back yourself is a refund signed with this wallet's key, from the expiry in earmarked mode: closing or reloading this page, or wiping the wallet, destroys that key, and the page asks before it wipes a wallet that funded a cash vault. The seller or the authority can also trigger that refund, and nothing makes them. After the funding is submitted, wait until the node lists the deposit before you build the settle; the page expects the node to list the UTXOs of accepted blocks, which has not been observed against a real node. A settle is refused at or after the expiry, so fund with time to spare: the review of the funding says how long the trade has left to settle.

      Add signatures made elsewhere
      
    
      Paste the IPST text that impulse ipst sign (or impulse ipst merge) wrote. It never takes the pasted document in place of the one you reviewed; it reads the review again for the result.
