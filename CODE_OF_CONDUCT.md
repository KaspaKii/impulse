# Code of Conduct

Everyone taking part in Impulse, in issues, merge requests, reviews and any other project space, is expected to follow the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1. The full text is at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/ and is incorporated here by reference.

In short: be respectful, give and accept review in good faith, and do not harass, discriminate against or demean anyone. Criticise code and designs, not people.

## Reporting

Report a concern about conduct privately to the maintainers through kaspa-kii.org. Say that the message is a conduct report. Reports are handled in confidence, and a reporter is not identified to anyone without their agreement unless the law requires it.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct reports. Follow `SECURITY.md` for those.

## Enforcement

Maintainers apply the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1: correction, warning, temporary ban or permanent ban, according to the impact of the behaviour.
