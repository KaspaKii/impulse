1. Standard mass cap, mining/src/mempool/check_transaction_standard.rs
   - line 26: MAXIMUM_STANDARD_TRANSACTION_MASS_PRE_TOCCATA = 100_000, a per-dimension cap (compute, transient, storage).
   - lines 29-33: STANDARD_MASS_RELAXATION_WINDOW_SECONDS = 30 minutes before the Toccata activation.
   - lines 43-47: standard_transaction_mass_cap returns the cap only while the relaxation window has not started
     (toccata_activation.early_by(window) not active at the virtual DAA score); afterwards it returns None.
   - lines 58 and 98: the cap is applied to compute and transient mass in isolation and to storage mass in context, only when it is Some.
   So a node whose virtual DAA score is within 30 minutes of, or past, the Toccata activation applies no 100,000 cap.
   The v3 settle (compute about 167,500 to 175,000 grams in docs/measurements/v3-zk-units.txt) is refused by a node where the cap is in force.
